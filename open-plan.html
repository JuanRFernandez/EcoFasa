ECOFASA is the sustainability committee of the Argentine Ski and Mountaineering Federation (FASA). Our plan is open by design: a small federation, a tight budget, and a method anyone can reuse.

What it is

A plan in areas: climate, biodiversity, circular economy, human rights, inclusion, governance and alliances. It lives in one shared spreadsheet, every change is logged, and this site is generated from it.

How to adapt it

  1. Copy the tracker. One row per action; keep the columns (owner, effort, deadline, KPI, cost). [pending: public template link]
  2. Pick your viable core. Mark the actions you can start now with few people and little budget. Everything else waits for grants or partners.
  3. Publish your numbers. Baseline first, targets second. Where you have no data yet, say so instead of guessing.
